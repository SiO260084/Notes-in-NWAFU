# Academic Writing

## Describing Visuals

When you write about visuals, it is important to analyze the graph.

- Recognize the type of graph you are given. Charts and graphs serve different functions and usually require different expressions to describe. For example, line graphs show changes in values over time, bar charts compare different types of data, and pie charts show a proportion of a whole value.

- Classify data properly and search for the main trend/key features/major groups/exceptions/other similar ideas.

Usually, there are three steps involved in describing a visual:

1. Give a broad summary of the information conveyed by the visual (i.e., a clear overview of what the visual is about).

2. Analyze the data in a systematic way, describing significant elements of the visual.• Visualize the information. Always present your analysis with specific data support.• Use grammar carefully, and with attention to any clues about time.

- Avoid vague expressions. Point to precise meaning by using descriptive, straightforward expressions.

3. Present comments briefly.

The following writing example describes the two graphs in the suggested steps.

#### Sample writing:

The two charts indicate the three major reasons for the decline of land fertilitiy and its various impacts on three areas.

As can be seen from the first pie chart, worldwide land degradation can be ascribed to four reasons,among which livestock's over-grazing was the most significant factor, accounting for 35%. It was then followed by deforestation and over-cultivation, representing 30% and 28% respectively. Other unidentified reasons only contributed 7% to the barren land.

Meanwhile, according to the table which highlights the different situations in different regions, it can be noticed that Europe, with 23% land affected, suffered the most, largely because of deforestation (9.8%) and over.cultivation (7.7%). In addition, due mainly to over-grazing (11.3%), 13% of agricultural land in Oceania became less productive, while the situation in North America was much better with only 5% of total land degraded. The major trigger in this region was over-cultivation (3.3%).

To sum up, it is clear that Europe suffered most in land degradation although the dominant cause varies in diverse continents.

Tips for describing visuals:

- Don't be tempted to convey all data shown in a graph. Your job is to communicate the most important information about the data.

- Strive for clarity. Make sure that your description of the graph is visually clear.

- Strive for accuracy. Carefully check your writing for errors. Be faithful to the data without any subjective speculation.
